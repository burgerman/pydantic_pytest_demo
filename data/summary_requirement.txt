Summary Statistics
In addition, the script should print the following summary to standard out when the run is completed:
1. Shares Bought: Total number of shares bought
2. Shares Sold: Total number shares sold
3. Notional Bought: Total value of all shares bought
4. Notional Sold: Total value of all shares sold
5. Per Exchange Volumes:
    * For each exchange, the total number of shares bought and sold
    * Sorted by the exchange name
6. Average fill size
7. Median fill size
8. Top 10 most active stocks: List of stocks with most volume (in total shares traded), in descending order and including the actual volume shares traded in parentheses
